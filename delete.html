Delete your TBD Auction account

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Google Play and the App Store require a way to delete your account and associated personal data. You can do that in the TBD Auction app or by email.

Delete in the app

  1. Open TBD Auction and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password, check the confirmation box, and tap Delete my account.

Deletion is immediate once confirmed. This cannot be undone.

Delete by email

If you cannot open the app, email support@tbdauction.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my TBD Auction account”. We process email requests within 30 days.

What we delete

Your login, profile (name, email, phone), watchlist, bids, auto-bids, outbid alerts, pickup appointments, saved addresses, saved searches, notification preferences, and saved card last-4 digits.

What we may keep

Completed sale or pickup records we must keep for store operations, taxes, or law. Those records are limited to what we need and are no longer tied to a live login.

If you have unpaid wins or items waiting at the Lafayette warehouse, contact the store before deleting.
