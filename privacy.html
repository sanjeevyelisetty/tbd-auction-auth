Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

TBD Auction (“we”, “us”) operates the TBD Auction mobile app for local liquidation auctions and in-store pickup. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we collect

Account (name, email, optional phone, hashed password), auction activity (watchlist, bids, wins, pickup appointments), and basic diagnostics if you contact support. We do not collect precise GPS, contacts, or photos unless you later grant that permission for a specific feature.

How we use it

To create and secure your account, run auctions, communicate about bids and pickup, and prevent abuse.

Sharing

We share data with infrastructure providers (currently Supabase) and store staff who need winner and PIN details to release items. We do not sell personal data or use it for third-party ads.

Your rights

You can update profile details, change your password, and delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account). Email support@tbdauction.com for access, correction, or deletion requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Bidding is intended for adults 18+.

Contact

TBD Auction · support@tbdauction.com · or Chat Support in the app.
